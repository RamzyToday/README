## About

I help founders and small teams turn operational complexity into leverage.

My work sits at the intersection of AI, startup execution, and operating design. I’ve spent the last decade working across venture, startup growth, and early-stage company building — as an investor, operator, and first commercial hire in multiple ventures.

I’ve seen the same challenge repeated: smart teams get stuck not because they lack ideas, but because their execution systems are too manual, fragmented, and slow.
